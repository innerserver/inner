# Connectifi v243

Members see Forms to complete on Dashboard only when an open targeted form still needs their response. Empty, submitted, and closed forms do not leave a permanent dashboard section. Teachers and moderators have Create form and Manage forms buttons; results and closed forms remain available through Manage forms.

The builder includes editable questionnaire, signup, MUN registration, and feedback templates. MUN registration starts with delegate name, email, institution, first and second committee preferences, country preferences, MUN experience, roles of interest, additional requirements, and a confirmation checkbox. Senders customize all fields and choices for their event.

Added dropdown, email, phone, number, date, time, and URL fields alongside existing text, paragraph, multiple-choice, and checkbox fields. A required single-checkbox field can capture confirmation. Values and selections are validated by the server as well as browser controls. Existing room/grade targeting, one submission per account, private responses, results, CSV export, and backups continue to apply. Existing v242 questionnaires remain compatible.

Verification covers field validation, legacy questionnaire behavior, dashboard visibility, staff controls, and the MUN template. No full audit or live browser debugging was run.

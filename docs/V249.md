# Connectifi v249: shared grade rooms and pins

15 November 2026.

## One room per grade

Startup creates a shared Grade 6, Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 room. Accounts in A, B and C of the same year belong to that grade room automatically, including existing accounts. Updating a student's grade updates membership and active session/realtime grade information. Student accounts initially open their grade room when their previous selection is Main, missing or an old section room.

A Grade 8 room announcement reaches 8A, 8B and 8C through the existing room audience checks. Tasks, questionnaires and attendance can also target that room. Grade 9 students do not gain access to Grade 8; authorised staff retain their normal room access. Same-year friend discovery from v248 continues across A/B/C.

Existing section rooms and their messages stay separate and retain section access. Their private history is not merged or disclosed to the other sections. New grade rooms start without copying that history. Existing section-specific announcements retain their original audience; choose the shared Grade room for future whole-grade notices.

The new synchronizer is in `lib/grade-rooms.js`, using the same grade matching helper as friend discovery. Repeated startup reuses the existing grade rooms. Account grade values such as 8A remain intact.

## One shared Pin action

Room messages and DMs now show a single Pin or Unpin button. It changes the shared pin for that conversation. Authorised conversation members can both pin and unpin; outsiders cannot. The separate personal-pin button and "Pin global" wording have been removed from the active interface. Existing personal-pin records are retained for compatibility, but no longer appear as an active message action.

## UI proposals

Separate local previews explore clearer grade/audience descriptions, a compact pinned-message strip, secondary actions under More, an announcement preview and inline friend-request confirmation. These previews use sample content and are not shipped as application changes. The existing sidebar layout and account colours/backgrounds are unchanged by this release.

## Verification and deployment limits

Focused school, connection and security regressions plus class/form checks cover grade provisioning, cross-section discovery, grade changes, grade announcement isolation, preserved section history, and authorised shared pin/unpin actions. Proposal screenshots were rendered in an isolated local headless browser without accessing a signed-in Chrome profile. No full UI audit was run.

Pushing the source does not verify the Render deployment. The document-scanner dependency and photo exemption from v246 are unchanged; no scanner service or production mail delivery was provisioned or verified in this release.

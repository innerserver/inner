# Connectifi v257: Glass backgrounds and photo controls

17 November 2026.

## Photo persistence and preview

Appearance drafts now belong to the current account/session and preserve the selected palette, visual style and photo during previews and profile rerenders. Uploading a photo no longer snaps Glass back to the previously saved theme. Removing a photo explicitly previews an empty background instead of falling back to the saved image. Successful profile saves clear the draft. Signing out clears the draft and removes the account photo from the login background.

Global palettes and visual styles continue to take priority for their respective settings, but they no longer suppress each member's personal background photo. Saving still uses the existing profile endpoint and image-size ceiling.

## Clearer controls

Profile settings now use Appearance, Background style and Workspace background photo labels. The photo group includes a thumbnail, supported-file guidance, a remove action and a save reminder. JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 12 MB can be decoded and resized locally to a maximum dimension of 1600 pixels, with further size/quality reduction if needed to fit the existing profile payload. Invalid images are rejected, photos above 40 megapixels are rejected after decoding, and animated GIFs retain their existing 700 KB limit. A completed decode from a previous account or cleared/replaced file selection cannot update the current photo.

## Glass refinements

Glass uses a consistent dark palette for fields and text panels. Personal photos remain visible behind the workspace with a dark overlay, while cards retain a readable frosted surface. The sidebar geometry and navigation remain unchanged. Mobile photo controls fit their container. No new PPT, subscriptions or storage service is introduced.

## Focused verification

28 school/profile regressions pass, including Glass photo persistence through save/state refresh and four appearance-draft checks. Local desktop/mobile samples confirm photo preparation from a 1.8 MB image, preserved Glass selection, removal and the composer remaining in the viewport. Twenty-four existing sampled contrast checks still pass across six palettes. This is focused verification, not a full audit or live browser debugging session.

# Connectifi v259: PTM schedules and photo colour palettes

17 November 2026.

## PTM setup and booking

Admins can use **Create PTM schedule** in the dashboard's school actions. Choose a teacher, teaching subject, Grade 6–12, title, location and slot length (5–120 minutes). Add specific dates, each with its own availability window and optional breaks. Preview shows the number of full slots before saving. Times entered use the admin device's displayed time zone; the saved schedule retains that zone, and all PTM slots are displayed in it consistently across devices.

The server creates capacity-one appointment slots for each date. Breaks cannot overlap or extend outside availability. Slots resume after a break; any partial interval is left unavailable. Availability must be in the future and end on the same local day, with an 18-hour maximum. Each schedule supports up to 30 days, 12 breaks per day and 250 slots. The existing 2,000-event store limit and 12 MB school data ceiling remain; schedules count toward those limits. There are at most 500 stored PTM schedules.

The dashboard groups PTMs by grade, then teacher/subject and date. Grade membership includes all A/B/C sections. Students can see and book their own grade's schedules. Admins can select a student from that grade and book on their behalf. The assigned teacher and admins can see booked usernames and the class recorded at booking time; other students see only availability and their own booking. Each student gets one slot per teacher schedule and can cancel before selecting another. Teacher conflicts, student meeting overlaps and concurrent claims on the same slot are rejected on the server. Admins can cancel the whole schedule or individual bookings, including a booking whose student has since changed grade.

Existing ordinary events and one-off appointments remain supported. A teacher event cannot overlap a generated PTM slot. PTM metadata and slots use the existing school data store and authenticated, CSRF-protected routes. No new service, credentials, user accounts or account-password changes are involved.

## Background photos replace preset colours

Uploading a background photo now switches the member's appearance to **Custom** and derives background, card, text and accent colours from the photo. **Use photo colours** also applies this to an already uploaded photo without reuploading it. Previously saved remote image URLs must be uploaded locally before their colours can be sampled. The image remains visible behind the workspace, while content panels retain readable surfaces. The colour editor remains available, and members can deliberately choose another preset afterwards. Existing school-wide theme restrictions continue to take priority.

The algorithm samples a small local canvas, groups similar colours and selects a dominant colour and a sufficiently frequent colour for the accent. Neutral images produce neutral palettes. It adjusts the palette to keep text and accents at least 4.5:1 against the generated solid background/card colours in the tested cases. It does not train an AI model or send photos to an external model service. Existing local resizing, image limits, GIF restrictions, upload identity checks and profile persistence remain in place.

Custom colour drafts now survive profile rerenders. Restoring a saved palette uses the saved values rather than stale colour inputs. Signing out still removes the member's background from the sign-in screen. Removing the photo leaves the Custom colours available for editing. Sidebar layout and geometry are unchanged.

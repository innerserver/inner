Privacy Policy

Inner stores account details, contact details supplied during signup or account requests, messages, reports, files, profile settings, device type, and login IP metadata needed to run the private workspace.

Owner-only security metadata such as latest IP, approximate IP location, and device signature is restricted to the hardcoded owner account. Regular members do not receive this metadata in app state.

Admins may use reports, moderation logs, and account details to keep the workspace safe. File privacy settings control whether a file is visible only to the uploader and admins or to allowed users.

To request deletion or correction of an account, contact a workspace admin.
